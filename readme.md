# Daily Blockchain Transactions: Comparing Ethereum, BSC, Polygon, and Beyond

## What This Dashboard Shows
This dashboard tracks daily active transactions across major blockchain ecosystems, including Ethereum, Binance Smart Chain (BSC), Polygon, zkSync, Base, Scroll, and Avalanche. By analyzing transaction trends, users can gain insights into the real-world activity driving adoption across these networks.

## Why It Matters
Daily transactions are a key indicator of blockchain utility and adoption. They reflect the demand for dApps, the growth of DeFi, gaming, and NFT ecosystems, and even the scalability of these networks during peak usage.

With a growing buzz about the crypto bull run, this dashboard focuses on tangible metrics—not hype.

## ⭐ Key Insights Section:⭐
### Ethereum:

The transaction count remains steady, fluctuating slightly around 1M per day.
A slight drop is noticeable in the last two days, potentially indicating temporary inactivity or end-of-week slowdowns.
### Binance Smart Chain (BSC):

Daily transactions consistently hover near 5M, showcasing its strong position in gaming and DeFi activities.
A visible dip mid-month indicates potential network congestion or reduced activity.
### Polygon:

Polygon sees consistent daily transactions around 2.5M, though a gradual decline is observed in recent days.
This could hint at users shifting activity elsewhere or seasonality in dApp usage.
### Scroll:

Transaction activity for Scroll is relatively low (~100K daily).
A mid-month peak suggests a noteworthy event, such as a dApp launch or protocol-related development, followed by normalization.
### Base:

Base exhibits fluctuating transaction counts, ranging between 6M and 10M daily.
Peaks and valleys highlight its evolving ecosystem and adoption trends, potentially driven by new application rollouts.
### zkSync:

zkSync transactions show growth peaks above 200K, reflecting its rise in the scalability ecosystem.
Activity remains highly dynamic, which may be linked to early-stage adoption or experiments with rollup-based applications.
### Avalanche:

Daily transactions remain stable around 200K, with a clear peak in mid-November.
This spike could indicate subnet activity or a high-demand application launch.
